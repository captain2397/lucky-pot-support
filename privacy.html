Lucky Pot

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 August 2026 · Last updated 8 August 2026

This policy explains how the Lucky Pot iOS app handles data. It applies to the Lucky Pot app for iPhone and iPad, bundle identifier org.berkeleycommunityscholars.luckypot.

Contact for all privacy questions and requests: akash.meena2300@gmail.com

Short version

Lucky Pot does not collect or transmit data. Game progress and reward information are stored locally on the device using UserDefaults. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, in-app purchases, or external payment links. If a user contacts support by email, the email address and message are used only to respond.

1. Data we collect

We collect nothing. The app sends no data off your device, and there is no server that receives information about you or your play.

DataWhat it isWhere it lives
Game progress Your level, virtual coin balance and related play state. On your device only, in iOS UserDefaults. Never transmitted.
Reward information Your last daily-reward date and chest claim state, used to enforce one daily reward per calendar day. On your device only, in iOS UserDefaults. Never transmitted.
Support email Only if you choose to email us: your email address and the contents of your message. In our email inbox, used only to answer you.

2. No analytics, advertising or tracking

Lucky Pot contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of any kind. No third party receives data from the app. The app makes no network requests to collect or report information about you or your use of the app.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • No name, postal address or phone number. No email address unless you write to us yourself.
  • No device identifiers of any kind, including no UUID, no Keychain identifier and no advertising identifier (IDFA). The app presents no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No payment, card or banking information — the app has no in-app purchases and no external purchase links.
  • No usage, product-interaction or event analytics.
  • No precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar or health data.
  • No tracking of you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and no sale or sharing of data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. How data is used

The information stored on your device is used only to run the game and keep your progress consistent between sessions. If you email support, your message is used only to respond to you. Nothing is used for advertising profiles, credit or insurance decisions, or automated decisions that legally affect you.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose your data. There is no data to share: nothing leaves your device. Support email correspondence is not shared with anyone except where we are legally required to disclose it in response to a valid legal request.

6. Retention and deletion

Game progress and reward information are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them and resets your in-app progress. You do not need to contact us to delete this data, and we cannot access or recover it.

If you have emailed support, you can ask us to delete that correspondence at akash.meena2300@gmail.com.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EEA and the UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it, and we hold no personal information about app users other than support email you send us.

Exercise any right by emailing akash.meena2300@gmail.com. We will respond within the period required by applicable law, and you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Lucky Pot simulates casino-style play, is intended for adults, and is not directed to children. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Security

Because the app transmits nothing, there is no transfer of your data to protect. Game data is stored in the app's own sandboxed storage on your device and is protected by iOS.

10. Virtual coins

Virtual coins have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for money or prizes. They cannot be purchased, wagered, transferred or withdrawn, and they carry no financial value. Lucky Pot offers no real-money gambling.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top will change, and the current version is always published at this address. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
