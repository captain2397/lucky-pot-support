How to play

  • Each round randomly selects a number from 100 through 999.
  • Enter a three-digit guess on the keypad. Clues tell you whether your guess is too high or too low.
  • The target number is hidden during play and each round has a limited number of attempts.
  • Guess correctly to earn 7,500 virtual coins.
  • Hints and extra attempts are built-in game features. They are never purchased and never cost coins.

Virtual coins

Coins are only a score and progression counter inside the app. They cannot be bought, wagered, transferred, sold, traded, withdrawn or redeemed.

Correct guess+7,500 virtual coins
Daily reward+10,000 virtual coins, once per calendar day
Progress chest+15,000 virtual coins and XP, when collected

There is no player-to-player wagering, sweepstakes, lottery or prize contest, and players cannot send coins to one another.

Frequently asked questions

Can I win or withdraw real money?
No. Lucky Pot offers no real-money gambling and no monetary or physical rewards of any kind. Coins, scores, wins, chests and celebration animations represent in-app progress only.
Do I need an account or a login?
No. There is no sign-up, no login and no user profile to create. Play starts immediately.
Are there in-app purchases?
No. The app has no in-app purchases and no external purchase links. Nothing in the app can be bought.
My daily reward says it is already collected.
The daily reward can be claimed once per calendar day. It becomes available again after midnight in your device's local time zone.
My progress chest is not available again.
The progress chest is claimable once per app session. Fully close the app and reopen it to see it again.
How do I reset my progress?
Delete and reinstall Lucky Pot to remove game progress and reward information stored in UserDefaults. This resets the game to its starting state. Some device-local SDK configuration stored securely in Keychain may remain after reinstall.
The game will not launch or a screen appears blank.
Force-quit and reopen the app, confirm that your internet connection is working, verify that your device runs iOS 18.2 or later, and check for an App Store update. The app's integrated SDK retrieves remote content, so some screens may not load without network access.
How do I report a bug or request a feature?
Email akash.meena2300@gmail.com. Include steps to reproduce, what you expected and a screenshot if possible.
What data does the app collect?
Lucky Pot does not collect gameplay or personal data. Game progress and reward information remain locally on your device in UserDefaults. The app includes a third-party SDK that makes network requests to retrieve and display remote content. The SDK provider confirmed that it does not track users or collect user data. The app contains no advertising or analytics. If you email support, we receive only the information you voluntarily provide. See the Privacy Policy for details.
Is Lucky Pot suitable for children?
No. The app simulates casino-style play and is intended for adults. It is not directed to children.
